How to Access Subject Management

Step 1: Login as Registrar
  • Go to /registrar/login
  • Enter your registrar credentials
  • Click "Login" button
Step 2: Navigate to Subjects
  • Go to /registrar/subjects
  • Or click "Subjects" in the navigation menu
  • You'll see ALL the buttons highlighted above!
